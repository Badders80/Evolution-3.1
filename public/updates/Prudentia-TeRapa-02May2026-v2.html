Prudentia Holds Her Own in BM75 Company

THE STEP UP

  • Third place in the BM75 at Te Rapa on 2 May — just 0.3 lengths off the winner
  • Drawn barrier 1 with 54.0kg, Masahiro Hashizume in the saddle
  • Strong progression from maiden winner to competitive in tougher grade

Prudentia lined up at Te Rapa on Saturday, 2 May, stepping up to benchmark 75 company for the first time in the 1400m event. She drew barrier 1 with 54.0kg on her back and settled into a hotly contested race under Masahiro Hashizume. The mare found the line strongly to finish third, just 0.3 lengths behind the winner Overdrawn and 0.1 lengths off second-placed Merchant Queen. Starting at $14.10, she outran her odds and confirmed she is competitive at this level.

The result continues her steady arc since joining the stable. A maiden win at Te Rapa three weeks ago gave her the confidence to tackle a tougher field, and this performance suggests the step up was the right call. The margin was tight — the first six across the line were separated by less than a length — and Prudentia was right in the finish against seasoned opposition.

The Wexford Stables team were encouraged by the way she handled the rise in grade. Barrier 1 gave her every chance, and the lighter weight of 54.0kg — down from 56.0kg at her last start — helped her stay in the contest to the line. She pulled up well after the run, and the stable will assess her next target in the coming days.

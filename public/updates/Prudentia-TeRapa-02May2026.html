Same Track, Same Rider - Different Company

Strong Rating 75 test at Te Rapa, tricky inside draw, but reunited with her last-start winning rider

Prudentia returns to Te Rapa this Friday for a genuine test in a strong Rating 75 1400m, again partnered by Masa Hashizume from Barrier 1. She comes off a tough, professional win at the track, where she was strongest late and pulled up sound after a deliberately quiet recovery weekend. Her subsequent work has been fluent and competitive alongside her galloping partners, suggesting she has held both fitness and enthusiasm.

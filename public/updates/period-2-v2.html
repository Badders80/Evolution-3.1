Prudentia Breaks Through at Te Rapa

THE BREAKTHROUGH

  • First win for the Evolution Stables mare in the BM65 at Te Rapa
  • Masahiro Hashizume guides her home from barrier 8
  • Stable confidence rewarded with a strong, tactical performance

Prudentia delivered her maiden victory for Evolution Stables at Te Rapa on Friday, 17 April, taking out the 1300m BM65 in convincing fashion. Drawn barrier 8 with 56.0kg on her back, she settled into the race under Masahiro Hashizume and found the line strongly to win by 0.3 lengths. The time of 1:20.580 confirmed the quality of the performance — this was not a lucky win, but a deserved one.

The result came at her third start for the stable and followed a deliberate preparation. After the scratching at Pukekohe the previous week due to weather, the Wexford Stables team elected to press on at Te Rapa rather than wait. The decision paid off. Starting at $10.60, she outstayed Fleeting Star and Amusement in the closing stages, showing the turn of foot her trainers had been targeting.

"She's taken the next step up. Once you're in a maiden and you've got to go the next step — it was a good performance. We're gonna take another step up now into 75, which is never easy, but time also helps them as they get a bit more stronger, a bit more mature."

— Lance O'Sullivan, Wexford Stables

One of Our Own: Prudentia lines up this Friday at Te Rapa

  • • Prudentia heads to the starting gates at Te Rapa this Friday.
  • • An exciting Contributer filly is set to join her on the path ahead.
Prudentia (on the right) coming home strong for her maiden win on a heavy Tauranga track in May

Prudentia lines up at Te Rapa this Friday, 17 April 2026, in Race 7, the Sal's Pizza Hamilton 1300 (BM65). Barrier 8 and 56kg give her a workable setup in a competitive field, and she strips fitter for her Tauranga resumption last month.

Andrew Scott noted this week that the track has had some genuine moisture: "So we had a good bit of rain on the weekend, and then a bit of juice in the track as well. So it's all going well." That reads well for Prudentia. She won her maiden at Tauranga on a Heavy 10, making up ground from a checked start to win by two and a quarter lengths in 1:34.75. A track with some cut in it plays to her strengths.

Race Sal's Pizza Hamilton 1300 (BM65) Venue Te Rapa Date Friday, 17 April 2026 Draw Barrier 8 Weight 56kg Jockey Masa Hashizume Rating Flat 28 Stable O'Sullivan & Scott

Beyond Friday's race, the stable picture is starting to broaden. A number of rising three-year-olds are coming through their pre-training and are set to take the track in the near future. The most immediate addition is Hottathanafantasy, a Contributer filly from the American mare Whiffle, who has recently been listed on the platform.

“There's a lot of racing coming from now on too, so plenty of options.”
— Andrew Scott, Wexford Stables

She is a two-year-old filly by Contributer (IRE) out of Whiffle (USA), currently in her early education at Wexford Stables under Lance O'Sullivan and Andrew Scott. No race starts yet, but she shows all the physical traits of a high-performance athlete ready to make her mark on the New Zealand turf. She is available now on the Tokinvest platform from 670.88 AED.

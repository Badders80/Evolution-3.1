Prudentia Video Update from Wexford Stables

LATEST NEWS

  • Video training update available for viewing
  • Shared by Lance O'Sullivan & Andrew Scott on 12 May
  • No upcoming race scheduled at present

The Wexford Stables team have shared a new video update for Prudentia, providing insight into her current condition and training progression. This marks the first formal update since her acquisition, giving owners a valuable look at how she's settling into her new environment at one of New Zealand's premier racing establishments.

The video, recorded on 12 May, offers a visual assessment from trainers Lance O'Sullivan and Andrew Scott. While no specific race target has been announced yet, these regular updates demonstrate the stable's commitment to keeping owners closely connected with their investment's development programme.

"We're pleased to share this visual update on Prudentia's progress as she continues her preparation."

— Lance O'Sullivan & Andrew Scott, Wexford Stables

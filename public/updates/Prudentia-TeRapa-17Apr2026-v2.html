Friday Form Analysis

Prudentia lines up at Te Rapa this Friday, 17 April, in Race 7 (BM65 1300m). After a period of targeted preparation, she has drawn Barrier 8. We are pleased to have the experienced Masahiro Hashizume back in the saddle; Masa was notably impressed with her turn of foot during her last outing and knows how to get the best out of her.

The weather looks to be playing right into our hands. Co-trainer Andrew Scott (Wexford Stables) provided a positive report following the recent conditions:

"We had a good bit of rain over the weekend, so it's all going well.
— Andrew Scott, Wexford Stables

This is excellent news for Prudentia, who won her maiden at Tauranga on a Heavy 10, making up significant ground despite a checked start to win by over two lengths. A track with some "cut" in it plays directly to her strengths, and we expect her to be highly competitive as she works through her grades.

Race Day Preview: Prudentia Returns to Te Rapa

  • Featured Runner: Prudentia takes on the 1300m at Te Rapa this Friday.
  • Stable Addition: New opportunities available with Hottathanafantasy, our latest Contributor filly.

Prudentia - One of Our Own

Prudentia lines up at Te Rapa this Friday, 17 April 2026, in Race 7 — the 1300m BM65. She's drawn barrier 8, with experienced jockey Masahiro Hashizume on her back. He was impressed with her on her last outing.

"So we had a good bit of rain on the weekend. So it's all going well.
— Andrew Scott, Wexford Stables

That reads well for Prudentia. She won her maiden at Tauranga on a Heavy 10, making up ground from a checked start to win by two and a quarter lengths in 1:34.75. A track with some cut in it plays to her strengths.

Current field: TAB Te Rapa Race 7

  • Stable Update

The stable is building momentum, with several promising rising three-year-olds progressing well through pre-training and set to hit the track soon. The most immediate addition is Hottathanafantasy, a Contributor filly out of the American mare Whiffle. She is currently listing and shares are available to purchase on the Tokinvest platform from ~300 NZD (671 AED). No race starts yet, but we should see her on the track in the upcoming months. She shows all the physical traits of a high-performance athlete ready to make her mark on the New Zealand racing scene.

Prudentia: Performance and Progression at Pukekohe

A mid-week assignment at Pukekohe Park sees our Proisir mare return to the 1400m trip, looking to capitalise on a promising recent effort.

Prudentia heads to Pukekohe this Wednesday for the Mount Shop 1400. After a solid fifth at Tauranga, where she made up significant ground late, the step back up to 1400m looks ideal. She has stripped fitter for that run, and the expansive Pukekohe turf should give her every opportunity to find her stride and finish strongly.

The Matamata stable of Lance O'Sullivan and Andrew Scott has the mare in excellent order. Jumping from barrier 7 with Masa Hashizume in the saddle, the tactical plan is to find a rhythmic position early before being asked for a final effort. At 56kg, she is well weighted for this Rating 60 contest, and we expect her to be highly competitive at the business end.
